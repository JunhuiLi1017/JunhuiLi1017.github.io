Research

My background is in biostatistics and statistical software for genomics. My current focus is deep learning: I am studying it in depth and bringing it into my work on next-generation sequencing data from cancer, immunology and neurodegeneration studies.

Research Interests

Deep Learning for Genomics

This is where I am putting my learning time now. I am working through the core architectures from first principles, including forward and backward passes and optimization, with the goal of applying sequence and graph models to variant interpretation, regulatory genomics and multi-omics integration.

  • CNN
  • LSTM
  • Attention and Transformers
  • Graph neural networks

Model Selection Software

Stepwise and best-subset regression across linear, logistic, Cox, Poisson, Gamma and negative binomial models, validated against SAS.

Nucleic Acid Thermodynamics

Genome-wide melting temperature profiling returned as GRanges, so Tm can sit beside ATAC-seq, RNA-seq and ChIP-seq signal as a quantitative feature.

Variant Calling Pipelines

Germline, somatic and mosaic variant detection from whole-genome and deep targeted sequencing, built as reproducible Snakemake workflows.

Tumor Immunology

Transcriptomic analysis for studies of cellular senescence, the SASP, STING signaling and immunotherapy response in pancreatic and prostate cancer.

High-Throughput Screens and Single-Cell QC

Zeta statistics for scoring hits in multi-dimensional RNAi screens and separating high-quality cells from damaged ones in scRNA-seq.

Quantitative Genetics

Joint QTL mapping, GWAS and genomic selection, from maize and triticale breeding populations to pleiotropic loci.
